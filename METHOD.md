# Method: standards every tracker follows

## Status labels
- **confirmed**: a primary record (filing, court document, regulator or agency action, official dataset, company statement) or reputable reporting that cites one.
- **reported**: a single outlet, unnamed sources, estimates, claims in talks or in dispute without a denial.
- **disputed**: credible reporting publicly denied by the subject. Show both sides.

Only confirmed items enter the ledger. If a reported item is later confirmed, update it in place instead of posting again.

## Evidence tiers (optional, recommended for conflict, human-rights and disaster topics)
Add a tier to the source list in `sources.md` and cite it in the entry text:
1. **Adjudicated or official finding**: court judgment, UN commission or inspector finding, official investigation report.
2. **Primary documentation**: satellite imagery with analysis, filings, verified video with named verifier, government datasets, on-record official statements.
3. **Independent reporting citing documentation**: major outlets and NGOs that show their evidence.
4. **Single-source or unverified claims**: label `reported`; never place in the ledger.

## Numbers
- If sources disagree, omit the number and say they disagree. Name who publishes each figure.
- Casualty, damage and loss figures: always attribute ("according to ...") and give the date of the count.
- Sums sought in lawsuits are claims. Fines and settlements are confirmed only when announced by the body that imposed or agreed to them.

## People and allegations
- People are "charged" or "accused" until convicted or found liable. Use "alleged" for conduct not yet adjudicated.
- Do not publish private individuals' personal details. Do not publish images of private individuals, or anything implying guilt.

## Sensitive topics (atrocities, violence, disasters)
- No graphic imagery. Describe facts plainly and with dignity for victims.
- Prefer naming institutions and responsible parties over speculating on motive.
- Record what is documented and by whom; say what is not yet known.
- Treat every side's claims with the same evidentiary test.

## Writing
- Own words. Any quotation under 15 words, at most one per source.
- Neutral, plain tone. The facts carry the point; do not add adjectives to supply the angle.
- Never invent or guess a URL. Use only pages actually opened or seen in results.

## Corrections
Fix in place, keep the permanent ID, append " (Corrected YYYY-MM-DD: what changed.)".

## Late catches
If a verified older item is missing, add it with its true event date and note in the report that it was a late catch and how it was found. Always search the feed and archive for the subject before adding.
